Just four films for October. 

### The Toxic Avenger

**The Toxic Avenger** has the right amount of knowing winks for the volume of schlock.

The movie follows Peter Dinklage as a janitor turned mutant, who wants to save his town from the evil, local corporation. It’s over the top, silly, cringey, and at times bad. It’s still a lot of fun if you enjoy something that is aiming at this level. 

Enjoyable.

### One Battle After Another

Paul Thomas Anderson delivers another masterpiece in **One Battle After Another**.

The film moves from an extended prologue of our lead’s revolutionary past, robbing and stealing as part of a counter-culture group, into the main plot of people’s past catching up with them seamlessly. 

The movie is fast-paced, switching between the various swirling plot strands, and coming together in a final crash. 

Very good.
